That denser or edge-matched materials can replace Pb is established (AbuAlRoos et al. 2019; Yaffe et al. 1991; Schöpf and Pichler 2016); the map shows the lead-free requirement is free on the thickness basis at every energy and for every polyenergetic profile tested.
