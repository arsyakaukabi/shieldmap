# Scarcity-proxy threshold curve

*Each number keeps its source row of the headline table (`data/phase4b/headline_table_v3.csv`) in square brackets. Unless stated otherwise all values are narrow-beam, polymorph rule R2, coherent scattering included, frontier kind best_clean, and prices are B4-internal with thickness at the measured-density point.*

The scarcity proxy requires every constituent element to reach a crustal abundance threshold.
Over thresholds from 0.1 to 10 ppm the thickness price at Cs-137 ranges from 23 to 215 % and at Co-60 from 20 to 160 %; on the areal-mass basis it ranges from 3.8 to 28 % at Cs-137. [H95:lo, H95:hi, H97:lo, H97:hi, H96:lo, H96:hi]
The curve is a staircase: each step is the threshold at which one load-bearing element leaves the set.
W (1.25 ppm) and Ta (2.0 ppm) set the steps that matter for the B6 leader (main text, Table 2); Ir (0.001 ppm), Bi (0.0085 ppm) and Hf (3.0 ppm) set others, and crustal abundances differ between compilations by factors of two to three. [H166, H167, H313, H312, H314]
We found no prior analysis of how attainable shielding thickness changes with an element-abundance threshold.
Under the scarcity proxy the practical leader is W, the established Pb substitute (AbuAlRoos et al. 2019); we found no prior estimate of how much thickness such a requirement adds.

## Leader of the abundant set at other thresholds

W (crustal abundance 1.25 ppm [H166]) leads the abundant set at the 0.5 and 1 ppm thresholds (P(best) 1.00 at both
sources [H164, H165]); at 2 ppm W leaves the set and Ta (2.0 ppm [H167]) leads with P(best) 0.98 (Cs-137) and 0.98
(Co-60) [H162, H163]; over the polymorph rules 0.56–0.98 and 0.55–0.98 [H162:lo, H162:hi, H163:lo, H163:hi].

## Transparent-set leader under the other polymorph rules

- At Cs-137, BiOF still leads the transparent set when all polymorphs are kept (P(best) 0.95) [H179].
- When the lowest-energy entry is kept, HfO₂ leads in its high-pressure Pbca form (0.60) [H90:lo]; the main-text
  Table 2 note b keeps this case.
- At Co-60, HfO₂ leads under all three rules (0.69–0.96) [H93:lo, H93:hi].
